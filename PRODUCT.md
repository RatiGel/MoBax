# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Georgian consumers (EN/KA bilingual) shopping for mobile accessories — phone cases, chargers, cables, screen protectors, audio. They arrive on phones in short, transactional sessions: find a product for their specific device, judge whether it's trustworthy and worth the price, and check out fast. Many are wary of the cheap-knockoff marketplace experience and are looking for a seller that feels legitimate. A second user group is the shop operator/admin managing catalog, orders, inventory, and analytics through the admin panel.

## Product Purpose

MoBax is a bilingual mobile-accessories e-commerce storefront for the Georgia market. It exists to make buying accessories feel trustworthy and effortless: clear product information, honest reviews, a frictionless cart→checkout→order flow, and local payment methods (Flitt/Georgian banks, cash on delivery). Success = a shopper finds the right accessory for their device and completes a purchase with confidence, and the operator runs the catalog and fulfills orders without friction.

## Brand Personality

**Modern, trusted, clean.** Calm and confident, not loud. The interface should feel like a legitimate premium retailer — Apple-store restraint, dbrand's confidence in its product, Aesop/Allbirds' editorial quietness — adapted to a value-conscious Georgian market. Voice is plain and helpful in both English and Georgian: never hypey, never fake-urgent. Trust is earned through clarity (real specs, real reviews, real photos) rather than persuasion tricks.

## Anti-references

- **Cheap AliExpress / generic marketplace.** No cluttered grids, no red SALE badges everywhere, no countdown timers or manufactured urgency. The single biggest thing MoBax must NOT look like.
- **Generic SaaS / AI-startup landing.** No gradient-text heroes, no identical icon-card grids, no tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section.
- **Sterile big-box electronics / carrier store.** Not soulless all-white spec-sheets with no personality.
- **Overdesigned / loud.** No glassmorphism-everywhere, no animation for its own sake, no attention-grabbing that distracts from the product and the purchase.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust through clarity, not persuasion.** Every screen earns confidence with honest information — real specs, real reviews, accurate photos, transparent pricing and shipping — instead of urgency tricks or hype.
2. **The product is the hero.** Photography and the item itself carry the page. Chrome, decoration, and UI recede so the accessory and its fit-for-your-device story lead.
3. **Fast path to checkout.** Shoppers are in short transactional sessions on mobile. Remove friction at every step; the cart→checkout→order flow is the spine and must stay frictionless.
4. **Bilingual parity.** EN and KA are first-class equals. Georgian text (BPG Nino / Noto Sans Georgian) must render and wrap as cleanly as Latin; no truncation or layout breakage as language switches.
5. **Quiet confidence.** Restraint over noise. One cobalt accent against a neutral surface; motion and color used deliberately and sparingly, never to grab attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (light and dark mode tested independently), large text ≥3:1, visible cobalt focus rings (never silently removed), full keyboard navigation, descriptive labels on icon-only controls, color never the sole signal for state. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` on all animation. Both locales (EN/KA) must meet these targets, including Georgian-script legibility at large Dynamic Type sizes.
